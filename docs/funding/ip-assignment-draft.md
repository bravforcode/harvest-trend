## Background

A. The Assignor developed the software, data pipelines, brand assets and documentation of the project known as Harvest Trend (รู้ทันราคา), listed in Schedule A ("**Project**").

B. The Assignor wishes to transfer ownership of the Project's Intellectual Property to the Company, so that the Company can own its core technology for its business and for applications to public funding.

## 1. Definitions

"**Intellectual Property**" means copyright, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, database rights, design rights and all related rights in the Project, whether registered or not, anywhere in the world.

"**Project Materials**" means the source code, documentation, data models, designs, logos and other items in Schedule A, including versions created before the date of this Agreement.

## 2. Assignment

2.1 The Assignor assigns to the Company, with effect from the date of this Agreement, all Intellectual Property in the Project Materials, with full title and the right to sue for past infringement.

2.2 The Assignor waives any moral rights in the Project Materials to the extent the law allows.

2.3 **Alternative (if assignment is not agreed):** The Assignor grants the Company an exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, modify, distribute and sublicense the Project Materials for the term of [TERM]. [Delete this clause if 2.1 applies.]

4.2 The Assignor confirms that the Project has no licence file yet. The Company will decide the licence for its own code before public release.

## 5. Prior commitments

5.1 The Assignor confirms that no other person, including any prize organiser, co-developer, funder or employer, has a claim to the Project Materials. [Check the EEC-SDG Hackathon 2026 prize terms and any employer policy before signing.]

## 6. Warranties

The Assignor warrants that:
(a) the Project Materials are the Assignor's original work, or are used under licence;
(b) the Assignor has not previously assigned or licensed the Project Materials to anyone else;
(c) the Project Materials do not knowingly infringe anyone's rights.

## 7. Further assurance

The Assignor will sign any document and take any action the Company reasonably needs to record or enforce the assignment, including with the Department of Intellectual Property and funding bodies.
